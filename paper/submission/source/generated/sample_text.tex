After support, quote, trading-activity, and required-control filters, the principal sample has 485,800 observations on 202 securities and 3,585 observed dates. The raw spread median is 0.157\%, while its winsorized mean is 0.473\%. The content index has sample standard deviation 0.710 on its 0--100 scale; after residualizing against the principal controls and fixed effects, its standard deviation is 0.297. Sample construction and sector composition are reported in the supplementary material.
