The principal estimation sample contains 485,800 security-days on 202 securities from 197 distinct company names. On the common-sample nested comparison, the content coefficient is 0.0041 with security/month effects and -0.0033 with security, market-day, and sector-month effects. The principal estimate with prior-session controls is -0.0014 (sector/date-clustered $p=0.533$). The contrast shows that the positive monthly association is sensitive to the treatment of sector-level conditions. It does not establish that misinformation has no effect on liquidity: the conditional comparison, content measurement, and uncertainty estimates all have substantive limits.
